ISS2K PC Port WIP

Football Focus

Nicola Cusano, best known for their incredible ISS2K mods and ROM hacks, has shown off their latest footage of a PC port they are working on based on International Superstar Soccer 2000.

I was first made aware of the ongoing PC port back in July, when Nicola showed off preliminary work that was anything but visually pleasing. Since then, the modder has been able to successfully get the game up and running in a playable state with AI assistance, with some nice extra features, such as wide screen support and remapped controls thrown in too.

 

Good news, guys!

The PC port is moving forward. I’ve now basically reached the kick-off stage of a Friendly Match, which I’d estimate is around 8–10% of the entire game. I didn’t expect to get this far so quickly. Hopefully I’ll be just as “lucky” with the rest of the project.

Of course, I’m aiming to create a remake rather than a 1:1 copy. There would be no point in recreating the exact same game.

There is a lot of potential here, and not just in terms of graphics. We’ll see where all these discoveries lead.

Audio is still problematic for now, as it is difficult to synchronize.

WIDESCREEN is achievable, even on ROMs used on consoles, for those who cannot play on PC. In the video, you can see the first tests: players no longer disappear during wider camera shots, and the HUD elements have been repositioned so they are less intrusive and make full use of the available space, just like in the ISS64 patch.

I’ve also made some very interesting discoveries. Euro Stars B actually exists—it was simply hidden, just as I had always suspected! It uses a mix of graphical elements from Euro Stars A and Asia Stars, but it can be unlocked and played. The only thing that cannot be assigned to it is a unique full-size team name. It has to use one of the existing names because it does not have its own dedicated slot.

I’ve also discovered how to move teams from one group to another and how to change the number of teams in each group. I’m not yet sure whether these modifications are completely stable, but they do seem possible.

It will not be possible to include everything I’m discovering in the ROM version, but I’ll try to add as much as possible.

I may even be able to create an NTSC version, but I’m not promising anything.

Stay tuned.

Making the Assist

As far as I am aware, there is no public decompilation repository for this game yet, so Nicola Cusano appears to be utilising a somewhat similar method that which SonicDcer deployed to produce his fantastic Extreme-G recompilation.

With Nicola's advanced intricate awareness of the inner workings of the ISS2K ROM, as well as the various tools they have made to decompress, extract and swap textures as well as expand features, the developer has informed me that they are using a mixture of hand-made and AI-assisted code (not AI-led) for the tougher tasks, as well as the N64 Recompiled knowledge base, to recompile N64 binaries into C code and then compile it as a playable binary to run on Windows.

The findings within the code that can be ported to the ROM will also be made available in the future, but there is currently no release and no ETA as to when the ISS2K PC port will be made public.
