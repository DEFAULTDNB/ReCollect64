SSB: Recomp announced

Developed by SrBananaMan ~ Oct 12, 2026

No AI, No Probelm

Have you noticed the swath of AI-led recomps and ports recently? Have you, like me, been craving some human-made ports to swing the balance back? Well, look no further, because SrBananaMan of Harvest Moon 64: Recompiled fame and a new developer named Boopie have announced that their next project is the N64's flagship fighting title, Super Smash Bros.

This static recompilation will come fully fighting-fit with ultra-high framerates, widescreen, low input lag, correctly scaled UI for ultrawide displays and day-one mod & texture support through N64Recomp.

A Smashing Remix

In addition to this, the recompilation will support Super Smash Bros. Remix, one of the biggest and most impressive overhauling ROM hacks for the system, which adds heaps of new levels and a 30-strong roster of fighters, as well as new modes such as "King of the Hill", "Smashketball" and even a "Tag Team" mechanic.

The development of this recomp has been tumultuous, but with the help of Boopie, everything is underway to ensure a fantastically high-quality recompilation will be produced. I cannot wait for this one, so keep checking the site for news and updates regarding this project!
