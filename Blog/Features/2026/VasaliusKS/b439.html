A New N64 Game by John Roo ~ Nov 5, 2026

Opening the Valve

A brand-spanking new Nintendo 64 game has been announced via The Retro Room Games, which sees a blend of action RPG elements fused with traditional first-person shooter mechanics. Vasalius: Human Error is the work of John Roo, and if that name sounds familiar, it might just be because of his involvement with another release for the N64, an animal-based puzzler called Big2Small by MDSteele, which saw a physical N64 cartridge release of the Game Boy/Game Boy Color title back in 2022.

This new title has a mysterious aura about it as well as some very smart-sounding features that I'm certainly looking forward to checking out when it eventually launches. For those that are wondering, it is made by real humans without the use of AI! You can check out the description and a short teaser video below:

 

Vasalius: Human Error is a first-person action RPG made for the Nintendo 64. It isn’t a PC game made to look old. It’s built to run on the real console, with a stock 4 MB of memory and no Expansion Pak needed. It has Doom style 3D levels and sprite enemies, with a bat, pistol, rifle, shotgun and rocket launcher. On top of that it’s an RPG. You earn XP from clues and fights and spend it on four stats. Speed, Reflex, Grit and Charm. Charm opens dialogue options, the way to get the truth out of people and spot the infected. Each hub has its own mystery to solve, and who you saved and who you accused follows you to the very end. There are several endings, and one of them is very hard to earn. The choice is yours!
