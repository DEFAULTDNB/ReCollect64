Half-Life N64 Port

By ToxyCola ~ Oct 28, 2026

Opening the Valve

Valve and Sierra Studios' Half-Life was one if not the biggest game of 1998. Combining FPS action with puzzle-based branching quests and a story-driven narrative. The silent protagonist and stoic scientist at Black Mesa, Gordon Freeman, has to survive an incident that causes a dimensional rift, alien invasions, military cover-ups and unnervingly mysterious characters such as G-Man.

The game was a landmark title that set to benchmark for all games that proceeded it, yet it never made its way to the N64 until today. ToxyCola has dutifully ported the game to Nintendo 64 (by hand as far as I am aware) and even built a handy tool to automatically repack your legally owned installation of Half-Life from Steam or otherwise into a console compatible Z64 file!

Check out the repository for full instructions, and check out the video below to get a feel for how the game looks and runs on the Nintendo 64.

 

Half-Life 64

A Nintendo 64 port of Half-Life's single-player campaign: the original maps, textures and lighting, played with Valve's own player-movement code, running on stock N64 hardware with an Expansion Pak.

Current release: 1.1. Get HL64Builder.exe from the Releases page; it builds the ROM from your own copy of Half-Life (see Building the ROM on Windows).

It is built from:

+ Valve's Half-Life SDK (github.com/ValveSoftware/halflife). Valve's player physics, pm_shared/pm_shared.c and pm_math.c, is compiled for the N64's VR4300 CPU unmodified. Walking, running, air control, jumping, ducking, stair-stepping, ladders, swimming, friction and footstep materials all come from the real code.

+ Your own copy of Half-Life. The maps, textures, skies, character models, fonts, HUD sprites, titles and sounds are converted from your Steam install by the tools in tools/. No Valve game data is included in this source.

+ A new N64 engine written for this port, because the GoldSrc engine (hw.dll) has never been released as source. It uses libdragon and tiny3d.
