Zelda OOT DS Port WIP

Portable Princess Protection

Not content with bringing Super Mario 64 to every platform imaginable, Tobias Friedly, aka GameOfTobi is on a brand new quest to port the Nintendo 64's flagship adventure RPG title The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time to the original 2004 Nintendo DS hardware. With the latest remake of OOT having landed on the Nintendo Switch 2, it's perfect timing to see the N64 version get some source port love!

Running at a near constant 19-20 FPS, and with an additional second screen HUD presenting your items and maps, this port is all but completed barring some minor visual bugs and general optimisation. The original code that this project is derived from is the current work-in-progress N64 -> 3DS port by Andresac90.

Wonder-Ware

Build around hardware with no analogue stick and a distinct lack of buttons in comparison to an N64 controller, this port includes a DSi-specific on-screen analogue stick option should you not want to use the adaptive d-pad controls for aiming, as well as on-screen C-buttons that appear for you to press once you have your ocarina selected.

Will this port see the light of day? I sincerely hope so. I have noticed that GameOfTobi tends to show us his weird and wonderful proof-of-concepts, only to not release many of them publicly (unless I'm completely mistaken). The PDF, Apple Watch, GBA and SNES and ports of SM64 do not seem to have any release information surrounding them; however, his most recent console-based effigy of SM64 for Game Boy Color is available on his Patreon, and the port of Super Mario 64 to the NDS with multiplayer support is available on his GitHub repo. The latter requires building the ROM yourself, for legal reasons.
