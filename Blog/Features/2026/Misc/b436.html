Blast Corps Recomp Released

A Blast From The Past

For followers of the N64 Recomp Discord server, you may have received a rather exciting ping a couple of days ago. For those who didn't, the ping was a notification that Rare's 64-bit destruction-based puzzler Blast Corps (1997) has been fully recompiled by SlaveOfIDO using N64ModernRuntime and the N64 Recomp toolset, without the use of AI!

Built from the Blast Corps decompilation project, the 1.0.0 release features high resolution, high framerate, mod support and is playable by anyone on Windows, macOS, and Flatpak, who brings their own NTSC V1.1 ROM dump to the party. Other regions' ROMs look like they will be supported in future releases.

An Explosive Engagement

Blast Corps was Rare's first foray into the 64-bit era, with production of the game beginning in 1995, with a team of just 7 developers, including graduates, at the helm. The game brought destruction and fun through 57 explosive stages of vehicular mayhem and has only been re-released once since Rare joined Microsoft in 2002 via the brilliant compilation of Rare classics, Rare Replay, some 13 years later in 2015.

This game single-handedly ignited my collection back around 2008, when a friend overheard me talking about starting to build a complete cart collection. They purchased it as a gift for me for about £5, and after that, I started picking up carts regularly online and in independent stores with the proviso that it only cost me £5 or less, or that I could buy a bundle and trade off the ones I didn't need to recoup costs. This worked for 90% of the N64's catalogue, up until I needed games like StarCraft 64 and, of course, Snowboard Kids 2, which command far higher prices due to rarity.

Blast Corps was always a firm favourite of mine, having finished it a number of times over the years, but somehow I never got around to re-playing it on Rare Replay. There is no time like the present, so I'll definitely be diving back into the punishing platinum challenges again soon via this stunning static recompilation.
