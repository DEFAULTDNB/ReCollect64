SSB 100% Decompiled

Disassembled Dualists

As spotted by TSR Stormed on X/Twitter, the Super Smash Bros. decompilation has reached 100% completion. This landmark achievement means that Nintendo 64's SSB will be able to live through the annals of time, hopefully forever, 100% preserved in a way that every video game should.

As we know, a full decompilation is not required for a recompilation; in fact, even a partial decompilation is not required, as demonstrated by SonicDcer with the recent Extreme-G recompilation. However, with the game 1:1 reduced to its raw source code, hackers and modders now have access to the nuances and intricacies at a granular level, which means that it's easier to work with than ever before.
