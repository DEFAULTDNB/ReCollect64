Ape Advancements

Following in the footsteps of Akratch's recent Diddy Kong Racing AI-led web port and subsequent downloadable PC/macOS/Linux port, ThatGuyMcd has released a static recompilation of the Mario Kart 64-beating and incredibly nostalgic and popular monkey-driving game, for PC and macOS.

The difference here is that the game is being handled by the N64 Recompiled codebase, the same one that powers all our favourite recompiled ports. From what I gather, ThatGuyMcd uses LLM's as an assistive tool purely to speed up tedious tasks, but the majority of the code is human-led.

The developer is also responsible for an upcoming Donkey Kong 64 recompilation, not the one I wrote about last month but another one that I haven't touched on yet. One that was covered by Video Game Esoterica last month.

 

Diddy Kong Racing - Recompiled

DKR-R 1.0.0 is a cross-platform static recompilation of Diddy Kong Racing for Windows and Linux. It runs the original game from a locally selected, legally obtained US 1.0 Game Pak image; no ROM or extracted Nintendo/Rare assets are included.

Version 1 highlights

Accurate presentation: original 4:3 composition, 30 FPS cadence, audio, object detail and game behaviour.

Modern presentation: widescreen, interpolated high-refresh output, FOV, extended scenery distance, maximum vehicle detail and anisotropic filtering.

Controller-first launcher and transparent in-game settings overlay.

Keyboard/controller remapping, quick race restart and two-axis gyro steering.

Independent live master, music, effects, vehicle and ambience levels, plus EQ.

Save import/export/backups, checksum-safe Save Builder and launch-time Magic Codes.

Optional performance overlay, CRT masks and hot-swappable RT64/Rice texture packs.

Native virtual EEPROM and four Controller Paks.

Game simulation, race timers, AI, input polling and audio remain on the original timeline when Modern interpolation is enabled.

Playing

Download the package for your platform.

Start DKR-R.exe on Windows or the .AppImage on Linux.

Select your own Diddy Kong Racing US 1.0 ROM when prompted.

Choose Accurate or Modern and select START Diddy Kong Racing - Recompiled.

Supported ROM SHA-1 after byte-order normalisation:

0cb115d8716dbbc2922fda38e533b9fe63bb9670

Open the in-game overlay with Escape, F1, or controller Back/View. Alt+Enter and F11 toggle fullscreen.

Release files

dist/DKR-R-1.0.0-Windows-x64.zip

dist/DKR-R-1.0.0-Linux-x86_64.AppImage

The Linux release is always distributed as an AppImage. macOS is supported by the source/build kit and requires an Apple host for signing and final testing.

Going Ape

The recompilation does seem to run well, and the majority of people playing it are definitely enjoying it, but a few crashes and issues yet remain. Hot Top Volcano has a strange clip-out-of-bounds issue, and there are some odd graphical glitches here and there. Hopefully these can be amended in future updates, and additional features such as texture packs, mods, and online multiplayer could be added in too.

With lots of people holding on and waiting for an official Harbour Masters port or a 100% human-made static recompilation, it's always preferable to see the use of AI as an assistant and not a full lead, especially on projects like this. Just check out Graslu00's "AI Slop Port" playthrough of GoldenEye 007 for reasons as to why AI fronting these projects generally leads to poor-quality products in the end.

I'm looking forward to seeing just how ThatGuyMcd's DK64 recompilation holds up against the other recompilation by Rainchus and the DK Randomiser crew. As ever, I have left links to this project's repo and the latest download on the front page for you to try if you so wish.
