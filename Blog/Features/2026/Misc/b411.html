The 40th Anniversary Direct is Here! ~ Sep 8, 2026

A Timeless Retelling

Since the initial tease back in June, there has been very little released (read; leaked) regarding the upcoming The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake for the Nintendo Switch 2. My friends and I have been waxing lyrical about the original game since the announcement of the remake and pondering what extras have been added, what could be altered to appeal to modern sensitivities, or even what might be removed from the game that made our collective childhoods amazing.

Today, that changes, with a 30-minute Nintendo live stream that focuses exclusively on Zelda-related information, which should hopefully give us more than a few hints towards the much-anticipated game that is meant to be released at some point in the next three months. Below is a breakdown of the key points shared in the presentation:

 

+ Eiji Aonouma introduces the original game from 1997, the first 3d game featureing adult and child, features Goron and Zora.

+ A faithful representation using modern technologies.

+ Hugely upgraded visuals, atmospheric lighting, realistic water, material textures, envoking nostalgia for original game.

+ Updated camera controls for a modern feel.

+ Additional dialogue to add to NPC personalities.

+ Jump button, rather than automatic.

+ No notable loading when entering buildings, only when going into new areas or dungeons, to keep original feel.

+ Snappier, more satisfying actions for things such as combat.

+ Motion controls for slingshot, bow and hookshot.

+ No voice acting in-game, none for Navi or Link either, just dialogue text.

+ Characters are fully voiced in cutscenes (not Link?).

+ Massive draw distance in Hyrula field.

+ "Threads of time" objective indicating tapestry that fills out as you play.

+ Holding A rolls then dashes, recommended for travelling long distances.

+ Massively reimagined Castle Town, new areas and a free camera to look around, no 2D backgrounds in sight!

+ Day night cycle marches on no matter where you are, it never stops.

+ You can humm into the microphone to play tunes. Ocarina peripheral coming for you to play it into your game.

+ Release date November 5th 2026!

+ Zelda Notes app can be used with the Ocarina of Time Remake, features records of accomplishments in chronilogical order with screen shots, enemies defeated and more stats. "Gossip quests" as hint system. "Play Ocarina" has a playable instrument on the app, make your own tunes or play along, share with QA codes. "OOT Quiz" has 2000 questions. Transfer rupees to the app for use in-app.

+ Young Link and Young Zelda Amiibo incoming!

+ Special Edition Console featuring a black and gold dock and a green and gold aesthetic across the Joy-Con 2's, matching pro controller too, 29th october 2026 release date for those!

+ New trailer for the game showcases textural grass, leaves, water, clouds, and lighting that changes. New animations and designs for enemies, new cinematics and voiced characters in the FMVs add more story and emotion. EVerything is bigger and more realistic-looking, but has a slight

+ The game box will come with textured foil cover art.

+ No word on a gold cartridge!!
