No AI, No Problem! ~ Aug 30, 2026

Time to go Bananas

Donkey Kong 64, one of the Nintendo 64's most legendary collectathon platformers, has been re-Kong-piled for Windows, Linux and macOS by hand and without the use of AI. Featuring all the staples that make each recompilation project great, such as instant load times, high framerate and ultrawide support, mouse and gyro controls, and even mod support, DK64: Recompiled is more than worth the wait!

In addition to the torrent of QOL updates and the randomiser, there are several notable mods available day-one. The best ones included on the site are the "DK64 tag anywhere" and "Start with all Kongs" mods, which will keep everyone happy, as they address some of the biggest Kong-plaints players had with the game originally. Work is ongoing within the Kong-mmunity for further mods to be added to the database, which I have no doubt will flourish over time.
