Banjo-Kazooie Geoguesser

Environmental Enthusiasts

Are you a Banjo-Kazooie superfan? Would you be able to identify the location based on a cropped image of an environment from the bear and bird game? Well, if you think you've got the right Rainbolt-style acumen, then head over to JiggyVault's latest project, Where in Banjo, and see how many of the 20 locations you can get right!

In each round, you get three chances to score points. Each incorrect guess zooms the image out a smidge to reveal a little bit more real-estate, reducing the difficulty and the points you'll receive in parity. Get it right in one guess, you get 500; two guesses 250; and three guesses nets you 100 points. You can play one or two players, but can you score a flawless 10,000 points?

It's tough to admit that I'm absolutely terrible at this game, but I can imagine that Markus Kurko will excel in this, while JiggyLookBack and Remember 64 could have an absolute riot playing together on their brilliant podcasts!
