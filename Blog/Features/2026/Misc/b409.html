Seta GO! Proto Spotted

Checkers & Chess

Recently, an online listing from eBay user Bay Area Treasures ended for a prototype game that was never released on the Nintendo 64. The game in question is Ikazuchi no Gotoku Chou Kousoku Igo, a chess-like game that we also know under the name of "Go!", which was produced by Seta and was due for release around 1997.

This prototype is possibly a one of one, with no other known development cartridges in existence. Sadly, this one didn't sell either. The listing, which was originally listed at an eye-watering $29,995, ended with no bids and therefore no winners; however, the listing has now been re-uploaded and adjusted to begin at a slightly more palatable $9,999.

As far as I am aware, this game is currently undumped, which is a shame, but the fact that a super-rare unreleased game can surface leads me to hope that others (Rev Limit and Twelve Tales: Conker 64, I'm looking at you) might eventually see the light of day with a bit of luck.

With a distinct lack of footage or images shown or demonstrated on the auction, it begs the question, "Is there even anything on that cartridge?" It's impossible to say without some brave soul stumping up $10k and attempting to dump it, though most standard cartridge dumping methods do not work on developer carts; an additional 12-volt supply mod is required for the BennVenn JoeyN64, for example.
