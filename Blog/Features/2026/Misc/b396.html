In something that was not on my bingo card for 2026, I just stumbled across a way to experience Super Mario 64 on YouTube. No, you're not going mad, it's some kind of quick time event (QTE) magic by Rovertronic that allows you to play the game using your keyboard's A and D keys to change Mario's direction when an arrow pops up!

With the levels essentially already played out, you simply control the direction you look in as the action replays. The demo is around 4 minutes long and encompasses the first star on Bob-omb Battlefield and the first boss fight.

(View the world through Mario's eyes, press the buttons to change your orientation. Just enable closed captions to enjoy it!)
