SF64 PSP Port WIP

Handheld Heroes

Several weeks ago, towards the end of May, Daniel Welch announced that he was working on a PSP port of Star Fox 64. Yesterday he announced that the game is running at almost full speed on hardware, albeit with sound disabled to achieve maximum frame rates.

With the help of SonicDcer and his Star Fox 64 PC port, the PSP iteration has been progressing steadily, already showing huge promise on the portable machine. Here are some of the milestone updates:

Star Fox 64 is running really well on PSP now!
With sound off, Corneria is running mostly full-speed on hardware, and on PPSSPP (see video) it's even faster!

This is all still within PSPGL, and using only about 60% of the hardware at that... 👀

Foxy Framework

The aim of this port is two-fold. Firstly, as an exercise in porting, Daniel Welch (TheMrIron2) is learning huge amounts about the N64's nuances as well as optimisation for Sony's 2004 handheld. The second is to use this as a framework for more N64-to-PSP ports better going forward. Could we see Diddy Kong Racing follow soon?

A beta release of SF64 is just around the corner, and rest assured that I will let everyone know when it arrives. I have been keeping an extremely close eye on the progress of this brilliant SF64 PSP port and sharing Daniel's tweets regularly.
