DKR Decomp Hits 100%

With the recent news that Diddy Kong Racing is being source ported to the Sega Dreamcast, the latest news to strike Timber's Island is that the ongoing decompilation by David Benepe and co. has now also hit 100%, meaning 1:1 matching code, although the documentation and labelling of functions is still incomplete.

Though an incomplete decompilation can be used for porting to other consoles or producing a recompilation, it's always best to have the entire codebase to hand, fully annotated, to ensure a 100% accurate port can be built. Additionally, more interesting modifications can be devised and adjustments and improvements made to the OG code that could even be repackaged into the original ROM, thanks to having the whole ROM sucessfully decompiled.

 

Diddy Kong Racing

This repo contains a decompilation of Diddy Kong Racing for the N64. You will need to have a original copy of the ROM to extract the game's assets.

All versions are supported, and the US 1.0 version (SHA1 = 0cb115d8716dbbc2922fda38e533b9fe63bb9670) of the game is the default if not specified.

As of July 25, 2026, this is our current score:
