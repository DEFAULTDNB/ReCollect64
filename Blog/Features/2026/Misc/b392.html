MK64 3DS Port Released

Speedy Source Port

Nintendo 64 games are getting a lot of love recently, with a multitude of decompiled games being ported to a swarm of devices such as Android, iOS, Apple Vision, PCVR, Nintendo Switch, Dreamcast, PS Vita and also Nintendo's own 3DS, a console that feels organically ideal for N64 ports!

This latest port by EstebanPdN brings Nintendo's first 3D racer into the portable console's orbit, with native 400x240 gameplay, optional 800-pixel rendering on supported models, diagnostic dumps for bug reports (press SELECT), wide 5:3 and original 4:3 display modes, a dual-screen interface (bottom-screen race HUD), a touch menu, and multiplayer as a planned feature for the future!

The current version at the time of writing is 1.2-E1, which adds diagnostic dumps so that you can assist in EstebanPdN identifying any issues should they arise. The game is said to work on both 3DS (all models) and even the 2DS family of consoles. It should also work emulated, but why would you want to invoke emulator inception by running N64 on 3DS on PC when you could just cut out the middleman and hit up SpaghettiKart directly!
