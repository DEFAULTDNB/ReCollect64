ScummVM N64 Port

Mark Bauermeister Provides! ~ Aug 3, 2026

Point-and-Play

A brand new port of the ScummVM emulator has made its way to the N64 by way of Happy-Ferret, aka Mark Bauermeister throught the highly versatile Libdragon SDK. Version 1.9.0 of the "Script Creation Utility for Maniac Mansion Virtual Machine" is now available through their Patreon, with a multitude of games supported and even more being made compatible via incremental bug-fix updates.

The three files released include the AGI (Adventure Game Interpreter) and SCI (Script Code Interpreter), which also accompany the main SCUMMVM build. AGS (Adventure Game Studio) compatibility may follow, depending on demand. Take a look at the progress below:

Video Versatility

The Broken Sword: SOTT currently plays fine, but the vide needed converting to perform better. The FMV (above) demonstrates the re-encoding of video to display natively on the Nintendo 64 at full speed. Using the MPEG1 codec, the video shows rather authentic-looking animation running extremely well. Happy-Ferrt might choose to re-encode the video to H264, a more modern format, which offers far superior compression and efficiency, should the format work sufficiently on the system.
