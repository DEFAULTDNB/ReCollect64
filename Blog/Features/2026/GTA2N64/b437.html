GTA 2 N64 Port

By Sunshinin11 & AI ~ Oct 26, 2026

Grand Theft Artificial

Back in 1999, we had no idea that AI would be running rampant, porting everything to every platform possible with very little prompting, yet here we are, 27 years after the release of DMA Design and Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 2, able to play it in full (including the Mad Island bonus map) on our N64's.

The neon-lit sequel to the 1997 open-world action-adventure game GTA 2 also never officially made it to the Nintendo 64 after the various struggles and the eventual cancellation of the original Grand Theft Auto for the 64-bit system. This N64 port borrows from the GTA2_RE decompilation effort, which was also, in part, powered by AI.

In order to test this out for yourself, you will need to install Libdragon with its MIPS toolchain, supply a copy of GTA 2 for PC, clone the repository, download and patch the engine and finally build the 58 MB ROM ready for flashcarts (on OG hardware with the 4 MB Expansion Pak installed or FPGA devices) and emulators such as Ares.

Claude Speed Vs Claude's Speed

At this rate, will we see GTA III land on the N64 too? The protagonist in GTA 3 was named Claude Speed, but the speed at which Claude, the most prolific name in AI, has churned out projects has been alarming and has caused community-driven sites such as decomp.dev to close due to "AI-overload", the flood of low-quality contributions which stems from anyone with an AI subscription racing to complete existing decompilations and build superficially functional ports for clout.

Where some end-users will simply relish the chance to experience ports once considered impossible on their N64s, others will quickly identify the lack of understanding or skill that the majority of these ports contain as well as an undeniable absence of craft, effort and quality. With plenty of developers now hiding any collaborative agents in their repositories, at least this one is upfront about the use of AI before we even try it.
