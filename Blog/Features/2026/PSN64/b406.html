This morning, Sonic-Iso, a modder and software developer who is best known for providing hacking tools for the PS5, has demonstrated the an N64 emulator running on the PlayStation 5. Using a combination of ParaLLEl N64 and Cen64, with the assistance of Claude AI, there are the beginnings of an N64 emulator, which is compatible with both the PS4 and the PS5, but as the PS5 is a far more powerful system, it might be the preferred choice for emulation going forward.

Though it is extremely early days, the PSN64 loads Mario Kart 64 and Super Mario 64, albeit with notable clipping, slowdown and stuttery sound. Sonic-Iso states on his discord channel that he had been able to run Super Smash Bros. well too, so with work, this could shape up into a decent way to play N64 on your jailbroken (and unjailbroken) PS consoles.

I suspect that PSN64 will not load Libdragon built homebrew yet, but I am in the process of suggesting a couple of things on the Discord channel that could assist in shaping the fate of this emulator. Sonic-Iso also notes that saves should work, and he will be buffing this with save states and graphic enhancements in the coming releases.
