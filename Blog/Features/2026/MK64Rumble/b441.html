MK64: Shindou Edition

Shindou, How do You do?

Some time ago, I mused that Nintendo's flagship racer would have been the ideal candidate for a Shindou Edition back in the late 90s. I even went as far as to ask Litronom, creator of the hugely impressive Mario Kart Amped Up mod, if it would be possible, and we even got an encouraging response from none other than Kaze Emanuar himself.

Mario Kart 64 is one of those rare titles that carved the series a fresh slant on the existing franchise and brought it up to speed with modern racing tendencies, but there was never an enhanced revision of it for the Nintendo 64 that added rumble to the game, similar to what Super Mario 64 and Wave Race 64 managed to score.

At last, in 2026, one modder has righted that wrong, bringing us the spiritual "Shindou Edition" of Mario Kart 64 that we all deserved. YurtleP is the one responsible for this ROM hack and has posted a brief explanation of the patch that you can read below:

 

Nintendo went back to add native rumble to older N64 titles that came out before the Rumble Pak like Super Mario 64 and Wave Race 64 via the Shindou Pak Taiou ("Rumble Pak Compatible") editions but Mario Kart 64 was sadly left without official haptic support. Mario Kart 64: Shindou Edition Restored fixes this historical omission.

Patch Features

• Native Shindou Haptic Engine: Executes official low-level Ultra64 motor commands

• In-Game Options Menu Toggle: Press L on the Game Select screen to access the Options menu. A brand-new RUMBLE PAK setting (ON / OFF) is integrated

• 4-Player Support:

• Speed & Boosts:

Start Boost: Big rumble on green light (shudders if you burn out early). Mushrooms: Solid kick on use and rapid rumble bursts every time you tap the Golden Mushroom. Drift Mini-Turbo: Quick buzz when your sparks charge, followed by a boost pop. Slipstream: Light rumble while drafting behind someone, then a kick when the speed boost hits. Ramps: Quick punch when launching off jump ramps.

• Hits & Weapons: Strong jolts when hit by Shells, slipping on Bananas, getting shocked by Lightning, crushed by Thwomps, or blown up by Bob-ombs.

• Menu Clicks: Gentle tap feedback when moving the cursor and picking options p1 only.

Two Patch Versions Included:

Standard Edition (Mario Kart 64 (Rumble Edition).bps): Features the full haptic profile across all surfaces, including realistic physical feedback when driving along the sandy terrain of Koopa Troopa Beach.

Smooth Beach Edition (Mario Kart 64 (Rumble Edition - No Beach Sand).bps): Tailored for players who prefer a less aggressive experience on Koopa Troopa Beach. The constant sand-rumble on the main track surface is silenced, while off-road sand, grass, dirt, snow, ramps, and all collision/combat haptics remain fully active across all 16 courses.

All you need to enjoy this version of the game is a legally obtained copy of the Mario Kart 64 (USA).z64 ROM and the supplied patches.
