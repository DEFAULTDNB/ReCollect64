Resident Evil N64 WIP

The Zombie Zone

Brazilian developer ElectronicaVictor has struck again, this time demonstrating and sharing his latest port for the N64 for all to see. The version 0.4 beta of the Resident Evil port sees two playable characters, Chris Redfield and Jill Valentine, jaunting around the iconic mansion, albeit in a diminished capacity, as the game is far from complete and runs extremely slowly.

Interestingly, the opening cinematics are gone, and while the voice acting is there in sporadic and minimal fashion, the game is running so slowly that the voices are super-duper low pitch.

S.T.A.R.S in their Eyes

The port, which is in an extremely early state, shows certain core features being fleshed out, ready to be used in the final build. The map is available, the camera cuts work for moving around, and the tank controls with aiming and firing work a treat.

Notable things missing here are the opening cinematics, the zombie's head slowly turning around to greet you, any window-breaking jump scare sections, the sound effects, and the loading screens that do those little door-opening animations that are synonymous with the game.

STOP! DON'T, OPEN, THAT, DOOR!

Each character has their individual walking, running and idling animations, and it's interesting to see the pre-rendered backgrounds and objects on display on the N64, though I couldn't walk up the main staircase, only through it, and there are no interactive puzzle elements added in yet.

A couple of the zombies are wondering the halls, but the AI used to attch or bite and lunge is not entirely there yet. You're able to draw your weapon, aim and fire at them, but there don't appear to be any melee actions or hit zones on the undead enemies, so attempting to get a head-exploding bullseye is impossible right now.

If you would like to try the port for yourself, please follow the link to ElectronicaVictor's YouTube Channel, and support the developer by clicking along with the monetised download links.

I will be following the progress on this PC-to-N64 port keenly because, again, it's another title that I wished was on the N64 back in the late 90s. Will ElectronicaVictor manage to pull off these ports? Will he start a load of them and never finish them? Time will tell.
