N64 Spotted on Socket

Ready for Launch

While perusing my timeline this morning, I spotted something rather gorgeous-looking. Socket Launcher is a beautiful retro games launcher built by Depmots that contains content for UMDs, discs and also contains wonderfully editable cartridges to configure, browse and enjoy.

While I had previously never heard of this project, the material-styled and highly reflective N64 carts definitely attracted me towards checking it out further. Below are some video demos of the Socket Launcher in all of its glory:

A Fierce Frontend

For Android devices, this is one of the cleanest, most clinical front ends I have ever seen. The materials and models used are nothing short of brilliant, and the overall look is stunning. Built with the Godot engine, Socket should be coming to Linux devices such as the Steam Deck or any of the swath of Windows handhelds once ported.

You can find the most recent builds over on Depmots' Patreon page. It's worth supporting Depmots because this project is super useful for anoyone with a penchant for a tidy all-in-one solution for emulation on their Android TV sticks, tablets, consoles or phones. Mileage will vary depending on your devices system specs, so make sure you have something that can handle whatever you want to emulate first.

I'm always inspired by these projects that put such crisp presentation and customisation at the top of their priorities. Maybe one day I will get my very own 3D boxart version of the RC64 collectors app up and running, but time is never appears to be on my side!
