StarCraft64: ROTV V1 Released

Return of the Void V1 by Poto Sacre ~ Sep 15, 2026

StarCrafting Mods

Poto Sacre has been busy building orc-filled worlds with their brand new ROM hack for StarCraft 64. Named StarCraft 64: Reign of the Void, this overhaul reimagines the Warcraft III story inside the StarCraft 64 engine, with new maps, new assets, new music, and a cooperative mode to boot.

The ROM hack brings together the efforts of various modders such as FelixEylie, Tokatta; and Teflon Ron and GSO from the Starleague64 community, drawing on various original map creators' work to flesh out this project. Poto Sacre has pulled it all together and produced one neat package with very few issues!

 

StarCraft 64: Return of the Void

After quite a lot of work, I’m happy to finally release StarCraft 64 — Reign of the Void V1!

It’s a complete StarCraft 64 ROM hack featuring the first three episodes of FelixEylie’s Reign of the Void campaign, adapted to the N64, along with:

+ A new campaign: the first three episodes of StarCraft: Reign of the Void by FelixEylie — 26 missions, accessible through Episodes IV to VI.

+ 27 new skirmish maps, including Desert and Twilight tileset maps absent from the original SC64, as well as competitive and Fastest-style maps

+ 3 Tower Defense maps — accessible through Episode I

+ 1 RPG map: Resident Evil: One — accessible through Episode II

+ 1 MOBA map: Aeon of Strife — accessible through Episode III

+ New graphical and musical assets, bringing more visual and musical variety to SC64

+ Modernized balance changes to unit and building costs and properties

+ Various SC64 bug fixes and improvements

+ An optional two-player campaign co-op version, based on Tokatta’s SC64 Co-Op Mod

+ Compatible with original N64 hardware, emulators and modern FPGA consoles such as Analogue 3D and M64. Play it however you prefer

Everything is distributed as BPS patches only — no ROM is included.

Known issues: In D2i, the game may crash during the final cinematic. To avoid this, do not keep the camera focused on the fight between the two Goliaths above

Cooperative Choice

The Reign of the Void ROM hack comes in two *.BPS patch flavours, single player and cooperative, but each one must be created separately. Taking the original USA *.Z64 StarCraft64 ROM with an MD5 of 559F71B861F639B6376D891E3023414B, you apply either the single player V1 patch to get a ROM with the MD5: EE9B5CDA23A885627F2840F24027CE54, or the cooperative V1 patch results in a ROM with the MD5: 2504FE800C9FD4B687DF2A7E556ACD0E.

As usual, you can use the online ROM patcher JS tool by Marc Robledo to get you up and running in seconds.
