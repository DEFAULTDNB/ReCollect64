Flashback N64 Port

Ported by Old Pirate ~ Oct 16, 2026

The Quest for Identity

Flashback was first released for the Amiga in 1992 by Delphine Software, a France-based company led by creator and director Paul Cuisset, and featured innovative rotoscoped animation as well as an incredibly cinematic aesthetic that was quite unlike anything gamers had ever played before.

Based on the REminiscence engine, this port to the Nintendo 64 brings intelligence agent Conrad B. Hart's slick animation, sound effects, music and speech to homebrewers with flashcarts on real hardware and emulation enthusiasts who don't (it's recommended to use Ares to run any libdragon-based N64 homebrew).

Old Pirate has also begun porting the SEGA CD version of Heart of the Alien, the 1994 sequel to Another World (Out of this World) to the N64, though there is currently no music and no sound effects working yet.

If you would like to give Flashback or even Heart of the Alien a go for yourself, then please support the developer and check out all of the software available over on his Itch.io creator page.
