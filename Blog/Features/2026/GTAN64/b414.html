UPDATED GTA N64 Port WIP

Ported by ElectronicaVictor ~ Sep 14, 2026
GTA N64 Port WIP

Grand Theft Awesome

Brazilian developer ElectronicaVictor has begun the process of porting DMA Design's (now Rockstar North's) Grand Theft Auto (1997) to the Nintendo 64. Based on the super-tiny 24-bit MS-DOS version that weighs in at just 9 MB, ElectronicaVictor has stated that the PSX version would simply be too resource intensive and complex to port to the N64.

At the time of writing, V3 is the latest iteration I was sent to test, with fixes for graphical issues, 3D buildings, sprite glitches, artefacts and car mechanics. It has to be said that the game does not run well yet, not at all, with major speed issues plaguing the flow of the game once you get inside a vehicle and strange graphical bugs regarding NPC cars and trucks travelling backwards and the map distorting while you attempt to steer every unsteerable vehicle.

There is a long way to go yet, but from humble beginnings great things might come, should the developer manage optimise the code fully for the Nintendo 64. It is unclear if the code is some kind of wrapper or whether it is native code being ported to the Nintendo 64 via Libdragon, but the crash screens seem to suggest the latter even though a full decompilation of the original game has never been made public. Perhaps the in-game assets are being pulled into a custom engine? We shall find out more soon I hope!

Ah Sh!t, Here we Go Again!

GTA64 has been a pipedream of mine since I first saw it listed as "coming soon" in the N64 magazines of yore. With the announced title touting enhanced graphics, extra levels and exclusive N64 gameplay mechanics, the game was reportedly at around 50% complete by 1999 (around when the PSX version was released), but the plug was pulled silently, and the game vanished from all existence other than in a Spanish publication which presented a few details and screenshots from that 1999 build. I seriously hope that this port can be brought into reality in a fully playable way, because this would incredible to see come to life.

As a side note, ElectronicaVictor appears interested in porting other titles to the N64, including Resident Evil and Resident Evil 3 too. The future of ports to the platform seems extremely bright these days, with plenty of titles being made compatible with our favourite 64-bit console recently. We have had a number of "in progress" ports come to light, including Tomb Raider, Vexxon, Sega Rally and even Comix Zone.

UPDATE: 15/09/2026 - V5 was sent to me which fixes the steering issues, however the main bugs remain where the collision area surrounding the vehicles and objects are massive and therefore you cannot drive far before hitting invisible walls. For those wondering, no missions are implemented yet either, so this is effectively an early WIP sandbox.

UPDATE: 22/09/2026 - A public beta has been sent to me to share with you! Be warned, the game runs slow and a lot of features are missin or broken. I tested this on the Analogue 3D and real N64 hardware and the results were the same; it's far from complete or playable.
