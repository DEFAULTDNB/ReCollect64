Comix Zone 64 Released

WIP Port by Frogbull ~ Aug 31, 2026

Classic Comic Capers

This morning Frogbull shared news that the 1995 side-scrolling beat 'em up game Comix Zone is being lovingly ported to the Nintendo 64 via the Libdragon SDK under the guise of Comix Zone 64. With the first page done, the second page is also shaping up nicely, and Frogbull assures me that any slowdowns will be addressed in the coming updates!

Rather than simply porting the game across 4th gen consoles, from Mega Drive to SNES for example, Frogbull decided to leap from 4th gen to 5th gen, bringing the game directly to the Nintendo 64. During development, the 4 MB expansion pak was helpful in getting this up and running, but Frogbull has since entirely removed the necessity of requiring an expansion pak to play it.

You can check out the footage below, but Comix Zone 64 has all the sound effects and hand-drawn comic panel aesthetics perfectly intact. With around 30-40 minutes of core gameplay in the full game, Sketch Turner has never looked (or sounded) so at home on a 64-bit console. Check back for updates as they arrive!

 

WHAT THE ?@!#?

Yeah, this is Comix Zone running on the Nintendo 64. 😜

Just like with Panzer Dragoon, I created this by decompiling with Ghidra the PC version I first played back in the day (thanks Sega for the Sega PC line!)

Why the Nintendo 64, the hardcore Nintendon't fans will ask? 🤔

To be honest, I originally planned to do it on the Sega Saturn to fix Sega's historical mistake of never releasing this masterpiece on their 32-bit console. But I decided to start with the N64 instead. It has more RAM, which makes development simpler, and if it helps chip away at that age-old reputation of the N64 being "bad at 2D", then that's a nice bonus.

This port was made possible largely thanks to LibDragon. The many modules included in the SDK make the work so much easier. A huge thank you to every contributor to the project! 🙏

Fans of the original Mega Drive/Genesis version will probably notice that the music sounds a lot different.

That's because of the conversion to XM64 (a MIDI-like format that works really well on the N64). I'll need to convert all the instruments properly so it sounds right again. That's one of my goals for future versions.

Right now only the first page is fully complete. The rest will come later. I still have a few issues to fix on page two, but it's already taking shape, so for v0.2 I expect to have at least two playable pages.

One thing I decided to skip for now is the really nice water effect in the sewers.

I fully decompiled it, but I'll need to find a way to recreate it on the N64 because in its current form it tanks performance pretty hard.

Speaking of performance: of course the game doesn't run at 60 FPS, and yes, it shows. I'm sure the Internet experts in the YouTube/Reddit comments will be quick to point that out (though there's little chance they'll actually read this post, they're too busy spamming their highly insightful remarks elsewhere. Whatever would we do without them? 😆)

So this is version 0.1, and there are still plenty of levels left to integrate. Btw good news for those without the Expansion Pak, I worked hard to optimize the game so the Expansion Pak isn't required!

There's still a lot to improve, and I'll need several optimization passes. For a future Saturn port that will be mandatory anyway... because yes, I will port it to the Saturn later.

A big thank you to everyone who leaves encouraging comments on social media, and an enormous thank you to all of you supporting me on Patreon. 🙌

Thanks a lot guys, and enjoy the game!

PS: I'd really prefer if the ROM isn't re-uploaded elsewhere 😉
