Comix Zone 64 Released

Ported by Frogbull ~ Aug 31, 2026

Classic Comic Capers

This morning Frogbull shared news that the 1995 side-scrolling beat 'em up game Comix Zone has been lovingly ported to the Nintendo 64 via the Libdragon SDK under the guise of Comix Zone 64.

Rather than simply porting the game across 4th gen consoles, from Mega Drive to SNES for example, Frogbull decided to leap from 4th gen to 5th gen, bringing the game directly to the Nintendo 64. During development, the 4 MB expansion pak was helpful in getting the port up and running, but Frogbull has since entirely removed the necessity of requiring an expansion pak to play it.

You can check out the footage below, but Comix Zone 64 has all the sound effects and hand-drawn comic panel aesthetics perfectly intact. With around 30-40 minutes of core gameplay, Sketch Turner has never looked (or sounded) so at home on a 64-bit console!

You can download the ROM by supporting the developer on their Patreon page. For the nominal sum of just £1.20, less than a cup of coffee, you can enjoy this wonderful port on your flashcarts today!
