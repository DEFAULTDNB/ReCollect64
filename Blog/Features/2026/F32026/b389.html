F3 2026 N64 Roundup

Kaze Emanuar's Presentation! ~ Aug 11, 2026

One N-Sixty-More

Kaze Emanuar, legendary Super Mario 64 ROM hacker and all around N64 engineering genius recently held his 9th F3 (Free Fangame Fair) presentation, which showcases the best upcoming and previously released game hacks and mods for a variety of titles.

As an N64-centric site, I follow this every year, and with the 2026 extravaganza completed over the weekend, here is a run down of every new N64-related announcement covered at the event:

Shadow the Plumber

The first "quirky SM64 Mod" shown was Shadow the Plumber Chapter 1 "Koopacalypse: The First Star" by indie-modder Astral Dawn 64. First shown last year at F3 2025, this story-focused mod follows the death and subsequent resurrection of the well-known plumber, who goes on a violent rampage across new levels with new moves, new music, and a decidedly emo new look.,

With a core psychological narrative of "What happens when grief consumes a hero?", this smaller-scale ROM hack is listed as "Coming when you least expect it!"

Looney Tunes 64 Mod Pack

The next mod shown was for SM64-Coop DX. The modification contains 11 of your favourite Looney Tunes characters, including Daffy Duck, Porky Pig, Bugs Bunny, Marvin the Martian, Sylvester & Tweety, Elmur Fudd, Wile E. Coyote, Lola Bunny, Foghorn Leghorn, Yosamite Sam, and Taz!

The pack also contains pre-recorded voice lines for each of the oddly-yet-iconically speech-impaired characters, so you will recognise them instantly.

SM64 Rogue Chaos Edition 2.0

The premise of this mod is utter randomness and.. you guessed it.. chaos. Each time you pick up a star, you get to chose from a selection of modifiers that patch the game is bizarre and unexpected ways. The game contains 200 patches that up the ante with new abilities such as big head mode, narcolepsy, money magnet, brawl mode, "unaffordable health care" or lava-proof shoes, dangerous challenges that include twisting the screen 90 or 180 degrees, zooming out to make the game miniscule, or cartridge tilting-like effects such as glitched textures, crazy mario physics, and much more besides!

Not only this, but there is now an "impossible" mode, and you get to gamble on a spinning wheel that either helps you or hinders you by adding 3 random helpful or terrible patches to your game. On top of all this, you can also partake in an on-screen game of Tetris to really test your multitasking abilities. This chaotic second mod was created by ArcticJaguar725, someone2639 and Anoliah, and is available now HERE.

Kirby (Mario) Air Ride

Built upon within the SM64 engine, thanks to the original SM64 decompilation, Mario Air Ride is another mod for SM64-Coop DX that adds flying vehicles and airborne battling to the expanded platformer. Commissioned by Scooter, this mod recreates the "City Trial" game mode from the two Kirby Air Ride titles as a Battle Royale.

Made by ProfeJavix, Drahnokks , biobak, @EspiDraws, MaybeNotJohnny, Teraok, @bl0cky64, @JennyDPeriod, EmilyEmmi, guest announcer Jeff Manning, from Super Smash Bros., and a massive selection of power-ups, Mario Air Ride will be available very soon.

Super Mario Odyssey 64 (Demo 2)

With the aim of rebuilding Super Mario 64 within the Super Mario odyssey engine, the mod, built by 1tsAJ is about to get it's second demo release any day now which appears to feature Hazy Maze Cave, Lethal Lava Land, Shifting Sand Land.

The mod was first shown last year in the F3 2025 presentation, and featured the Castle Outside and Courtyard, Castle First floor, Bob-Omb Battlefield, Whomp's Fortress, Cool Cool Mountain, Jolly Roger Bay, Bowser in the Dark World and 3 Secret Stages (Including Slide, Aquarium, and Tower of the Wing Cap).

SM64 Galactic Adventure

JackTheGothGuy has been busy making a rather gorgeous-looking Super Mario Galaxy inspired ROM hack for the Nintendo 64. Feturing mechanics, power-ups, characters, levels and more, the ROM hack is slated for a December 2026 release.

Quasi-Spatial Creation Nexus

Featuring the reimagining of 30+ mods in one game, Quasi-Spatial Creation Nexus shuffles levels across 200 areas with an interconnected map, new upgradeable power-ups and abilities (as well as old ones), multiple characters to play as including Luigi, Peach and Bowser, and has 250 stars to collect.

Created by CowQuack on April the 1st 2026, the latest 1.1 version of the mod is available today HERE.

The Legend of Zelda: The Glass of Fate

OOT has had a lot of ROM hacks recently, but this one was an odd showing. Made in Godot, The Legend of Zelda: Glass of Fate is designed by Justice 2 Free as more of a proof of concept for possibly bringing OOT-looking Zelda titles to PC.

Chapter 1 will release as a free download in February 2027, so it will be interesting to see exactly what this does and how it pans out. The comments on the YouTube reveal video show a mixture of adoration in the 64-bit style used within a modern engine, but palpable disappointment that this is not a ROM hack.

Paper Mario: Star Nova

As covered by myself back in May, Paper Mario: Star Nova is the fan game made by NicTube which brings a brand-new story across multiple chapters, new allies and enemies, and adds 70 new star pieces to collect.

You can download the latest Paper Mario: Star Nova patches from NicTube's Itch.io page HERE

Banjo Kazooie: Quest 4 Kurkoins

Last but not least, Markus Kurko wows us with his latest Banjo Kazooie ROM hack which is sub-titled Quest 4 Kurkoins. The mod has been in development for quite a while now, features 8 Worlds + Full Hub World, 90 Kurkoins, 1000 Notes, 90 Mumbo Tokens, 18 Honeycombs, and more!

Mark hopes to get it out to players before the end of 2026, but if you would like to follow his progress or contribute via polls or donations, then please visit his Patreon site HERE

Mario is Missing?

One notable omission from this years presentation was something I had hoped would be showcased and even released. Any mention of Kaze's own highly-anticipated SM64ROM hack Return to Yoshi's Island was entirely abscent. The last demo for the mod, named Demo 2, arrived in January, some 8 month ago, and teased a lot of fantastic features and clever modern programming tricks that ran perfectly on the OG hardware as well as withink emulators.

When I asked Kaze about the possibility of an announcement for F3, he simply did not reply. Let's hope it does eventually release, and doesn't get lost in the ether of space.
