GoldenEye 007 Plus Released

Source Code, Boss Mode

Thanks to the efforts of the decompilation project, GoldenEye 007's source code is being scrutinised down to the tiniest details, and with effort, new features and modes are being added by adventurous modders. Joshua-1248 is one such modder, who has taken on the task and produced this something quite special manually, by hand, as far as I can tell.

With additional features being modded into the game through backporting selective features from Perfect Dark and trial and error, such as split-screen co-operative modes and third-person view, as well as expanded and mirrored levels to bring fresh variety to the multiplayer mode, there is a lot to look forward to with this incredible modification!

(The cheats menu is overflowing and the ability to switch views and play 2-4 player co-op is nothing short of game-changing!)

 

GoldenEye 007 Plus — an enhanced fork of the GoldenEye 007 N64 decompilation, focused on optimization, expanded co-op/multiplayer, fixes, and quality-of-life features. Based on KholdFuzion/goldeneye_src.

+ Features:

+ Performance and load-time optimization passes;

+ Expanded 1–4 player campaign Co-Op architecture;

+ Expanded multiplayer maps, menus, character selection and dual-wield behavior;

+ Save-backed gameplay/Special Options;

+ Third Person gameplay with adjustable camera and crosshair tuning;

+ Third Person cutscene handoff fixes, including the Surface II and Dam end cinematics;

+ TP Sight Translucency while manually aiming;

+ Third Person projectile/beam/tracer alignment work;

+ Moonraker Laser Third Person straight-ray penetration work;

+ Shared campaign Co-Op mission-item and objective-state handling;

+ Co-Op mission report/statistics routing;

+ Mirrored Levels support and live-toggle safety work;

+ Basic/Advanced Map Maker development with a dedicated native Map Maker stage architecture;

+ Selective, evidence-based Perfect Dark backports where the underlying behavior is genuinely equivalent or appropriate.

Licenced to Appeal

I gave this a spin, and I have to say, I was utterly blown away. To see all of these features working on the Analogue 3D, upscaled and overclocked, is unbelievable. That fact that it also works on a stock N64 via flashcart feels like a fever dream. Playing in third person, cooperatively in mirrored levels feels like unlocking something you were told about in the school playground in the late 90s, except it's legit now! It's thoroughly fascinating to witness, and if you hadn't seen it with your own eyes, you simply wouldn't believe it. Just open your watch, navigate to settings and navigate the special option to enjoy in-game cheats, third-person view and a whole lot more!

To experience this astounding modification for yourself, you will need a legally obtained dump of the GoldenEye 007 U.z64 ROM (MD5: 70C525880240C1E838B8B1BE35666C3B) and the xdelta patch from the releases page on the GE007 Plus GitHub repo linked below.
