Gaming is Forever

I was 15 when GoldenEye 007 released, and I remember taking a bus to Toys R Us in Purley Way, Croydon, to go buy it with a mate from school. I remember us thinking, "What if they won't sell it to us because we don't have I.D.?" Thankfully they did, and the rest was history.

I must have put hundreds of hours into the multiplayer over the years, playing round after round on lunch breaks, "study periods", and outside of school hours. This game unified my friends and made rivalries that we had never comprehended before. From gloating over throwing knife or slap kills, crying laughing over successful proximity mine traps, or the absolute blind panic of running around with the flag in CTF mode, this game accounts for the majority of my fondest formative years.

With the GoldenEye 007 decompilation motoring away and extremely close to completion, I cannot wait to see what modders bring to the recompilation and ports that will undoubtedly arrive. I will be 100% there for it, just as I was back in the day!
